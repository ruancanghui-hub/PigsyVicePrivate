FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Data stays on your device. Export a JSON backup if you want a copy.

How do purchases work?

Pro is sold through the App Store. Use Restore purchases if you reinstall. Lifetime is a one-time non-consumable; monthly/yearly auto-renew unless cancelled.

Why do I see ads?

Free plan may show app-open and rewarded ads. Pro removes them. Rewarded ads can unlock extra habit slots on free.

Is this medical treatment?

No. Bajie is wellness self-help only — not diagnosis or treatment.
