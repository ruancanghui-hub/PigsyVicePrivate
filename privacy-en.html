Privacy Policy

Effective / updated: 2026-10-27
Product: iOS app “Bajie” (Bundle ID: com.nightelf.bajie)

1. Data we process

Bajie is local-first. Habit names, slips, points, gifts, felt progress, and settings are stored on your device (SQLite / preferences). We do not run a Bajie account cloud for your habit log.

1.1 We do not actively collect

  • Name, phone, or email (unless you email us);
  • Precise location, contacts, microphone, or health/motion sensor data;
  • Medical or clinical records;
  • We do not read existing Photos; we only write a poster when you choose Save to Photos.

2. Permissions

  • Notifications: requested only if you enable the daily practice reminder. You can deny or turn them off in system Settings.
  • Photos (add only): requested only when you save a hold poster. Denial blocks save; system share still works. We do not browse or upload your library.
  • Health / location / microphone: not requested in this version.

3. In-app purchases

Pro (monthly subscription, yearly subscription, lifetime non-consumable) is sold through the Apple App Store / StoreKit. Apple processes payment and receipts under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We only store an on-device Pro entitlement flag for feature gates (multiple habits, full practices, unlimited gifts, no ads).

4. Advertising

The free plan may show Google AdMob app-open and rewarded ads (for example a rewarded ad for an extra habit slot). Pro removes those ads.

AdMob may process device identifiers and ad interactions under Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob Help. This version does not force an App Tracking Transparency prompt; if that changes we will update this policy.

5. Analytics & crash (optional)

The project may include Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics / Remote Config. Data is sent only if Firebase is actually initialized and enabled. See Firebase privacy.

6. How information is used

  • Record habits and settle held / slip status;
  • Points, gifts, and practice progress;
  • Local reminders;
  • Gate features and ads by Pro / ad entitlement.

We do not sell your local content. Without a cloud account store, we cannot view your habit details on a server.

7. Sharing

  • Apple (IAP settlement);
  • Google AdMob (ads on the free plan);
  • Files you export and share yourself;
  • When required by law or to protect users and the public.

8. Retention & deletion

  • Local data stays with the app; uninstalling usually deletes it (per iOS behavior).
  • Export a local backup before deleting if you want a copy.
  • There is no cloud account to close — stop using and uninstall to stop local processing.

9. Children

Bajie is for people managing their own habits; it is not aimed at children. Guardians who believe a minor used the app without consent can contact us.

10. Security

Data stays on your device by default. Protect device unlock and treat exported JSON carefully.

11. Updates

We may update this policy and the “updated” date. Continued use means you understand the updated policy.
